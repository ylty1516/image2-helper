# Visual Case: Combat Action AI Flavor

## Source Observation

The four sample images are visually strong, but they show a common AI battle-image pattern: high spectacle can arrive before readable action design.

The clearest image is the close martial-arts clash because the viewer can quickly read the attack/defense relation, limb silhouettes, contact beat, expressions, cloth direction, and alley framing. The more AI-feeling images lean harder on glow, debris, fire, magic circles, neon beams, or monster detail before the action skeleton is fully readable.

## AI-Feeling Causes Found

- The brightest area often becomes an effect wall instead of a precise impact point.
- Weapons, spells, and energy trails sometimes cover hands, wrists, shoulders, hips, knees, or feet.
- The viewer cannot always name the action verb quickly: slash, block, parry, cast, dodge, thrust, kick, or counter.
- Hair, cloth, debris, sparks, and fire may move in multiple unrelated directions.
- Bodies can feel weightless when the stance, jump arc, grip, or landing/support point is unclear.
- Background destruction and floating debris can look decorative instead of caused by the clash.
- Detail intensity is spread everywhere, so the eye gets spectacle but not a clean action read.

## Human-Directed Action Principles

- Design the action skeleton before effects.
- Give the frame one primary attack/defense beat.
- Reserve the brightest value for the impact or near-contact point.
- Keep joints, grips, silhouettes, and facial intent readable.
- Make secondary motion follow one force vector.
- Use background destruction as consequence, not decoration.
- Keep at least one quiet zone near the pose.

## Copy-Ready Prompt Add-On

```text
Combat action clarity: one readable attack/defense beat, exact contact or near-contact point, clear force vector, physically possible stance/jump/grip, visible joints and silhouettes, hair/cloth/debris following the same impact direction, effects kept behind or around the anatomy, and one quiet zone for readability.
```

## Edit Prompt For A Flashy But AI-Looking Battle Image

```text
Reduce AI-feeling spectacle while preserving the character designs, scene, style, and battle concept. Rebuild the action skeleton: define attacker, defender, action verb, contact point, force direction, stance or jump support, hand/weapon grip, and reaction. Move glow, sparks, debris, hair, cloth, and background destruction so they support the impact vector instead of covering joints, faces, weapons, or silhouettes. Keep one bright impact point and simplify surrounding noise.
```
